authorship and notices. The Soviet-era factory and journal documents are credited
to their issuing institution/publication when individual authors are unverified.

methods; they do not claim authorship of the preserved references.

The copied domain-experts role is separately accompanied by its upstream
[MIT license](reference/guides/naval-architect-license/original.txt). It is not
installed or automatically invoked.

# Sarepta photographic references

Source: https://karopka.ru/work/019d46e2-b6fe-711e-8789-135416e3348e

Review by Billy Bones / Igor Sheynin of a KS 3D model kit. Downloaded October 22,
2026 at the user's request: 92 full-size gallery images. `manifest.json` maps every
local filename to its original URL. Four contact sheets provide numbered indexes.
The photographs remain their authors' work; downloading for reference does not
assign any redistribution license.

Useful images: 12 (plan), 20 and 22 (bow), 33 and 34 (bottom), 87 (full-size boat
on a trailer), 84 and 85 (finished model renderings). The model-kit images are
secondary shape references, not factory measurements.

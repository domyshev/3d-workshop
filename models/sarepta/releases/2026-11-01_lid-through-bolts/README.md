# Lid through-bolt joint

The two centred lid tab holes pass through both halves. At overall length 340 mm the hole diameter is 4.3 mm for M4 bolts. Each tab is 2.5 mm thick along the boat; the paired stack is 5 mm. The tab shape and lower hull retain the preceding design. At length 170 mm the lid holes scale to 2.15 mm; hull M4 holes stay 4.3 mm. See FASTENERS.md for all quantities and dimensions.

Exact raw exports are in raw/. Geometry checks cover closed meshes and through-hole material probes for both sizes; posed 340 mm meshes fit within 170 mm length. Physical print, bolt fit, nut access and joint strength are untested.

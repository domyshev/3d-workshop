# Current fasteners

For a 340 mm whole boat, with 170 mm nominal halves:

| Connection | Quantity | Hardware | Holes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Hull transverse joint | 4 | M4 bolts with nuts | 4.3 mm through both 2.5 mm flanges |
| Lid transverse joint | 2 | M4 bolts with nuts | 4.3 mm through both 2.5 mm tabs |
| Skirt to hull | 18 | Smooth studs, 1.6 mm diameter x 3 mm long | Existing blind sockets |
| Lid to hull | 4 | Vertical locators, 3.2 mm diameter x 4 mm long | Existing positioning holes |

Lid hole diameter scales with the lid: 2.15 mm at whole length 170 mm. Hull M4 holes remain 4.3 mm. The lid and hull bolt previews are simplified purchased hardware. Actual fastener fit and assembly have not been physically tested.

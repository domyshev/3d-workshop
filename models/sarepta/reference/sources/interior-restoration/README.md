# Interior evidence from an owner restoration

[Original article](https://motolodka.ru/boats/repair/sar_rep.htm), captured
2026-10-23. Text and photographs retain their authors' rights; no licence
is inferred. Original page and selected original photos are preserved.
The byline/email is obfuscated on the retrieved page; no identity is invented.
The text expressly credits the initial-condition photo to Vadim and Oleg
from Irkutsk. This is a restored/modified boat, not an untouched factory survey.

Most useful: `sar_rep15.jpg`, the real helm/dashboard and lower leg area;
`sar_rep11.jpg`, the open forward locker; `sar_rep5.jpg`, aft partition.
The dashboard photo supports separating the upper console from the lower
leg area but does not supply a 200 mm dimension or a measured stepped section.
That step is a plausible user-requested reconstruction, not a recovered drawing.
The author describes flotation blocks under the windscreen, in boxed upper
sides and beneath the motor well; owner-added foam must be distinguished.
